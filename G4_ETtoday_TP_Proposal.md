## 1. Background (Project Track & Topic Statement)

**Track:** Track 2, Platform Strategy Analysis (multi-sided platform ecosystems and network effects).

Taiwan's news business has split into two layers. One layer produces the news: ETtoday, UDN, LTN, TVBS and the rest. The other layer decides who sees it: LINE, YouTube, Facebook and Google. ETtoday is unusual because it sits in both. It runs a platform of its own, with readers on one side and advertisers and content partners on the other, and at the same time it is a supplier to the much larger platforms that now deliver most of Taiwan's news.

That double position is the puzzle we want to study. In the Reuters Institute's Digital News Report 2026, ETtoday is the most-used online news brand in Taiwan (38% weekly use, excluding the Yahoo portal) and 46% of respondents say they trust it, against a national average of 25% for news in general ([ETtoday's report on DNR 2026](https://www.ettoday.net/news/20260617/3184999.htm)). Yet the company lost money for four years in a row and only returned to profit in July 2025, after headcount fell from 561 to 385 as AI tools were brought into the newsroom ([ETtoday 財經雲, 6 Nov 2025](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3063136)). Over the same period LINE (49%), YouTube (48%) and Facebook (47%) became the main places Taiwanese go for news, and growth in a NT$66.2 billion digital ad market has concentrated in international platforms while local media's share shrinks ([DMA 2025 report, via Bnext](https://www.bnext.com.tw/podcast/2168/fs-cmtcsw21g0wdf01vrbdcvfozf)).

Our research question: does ETtoday's platform strategy, meaning its network effects, how it makes money, and how it competes and cooperates with the distribution platforms, explain the 2025 turnaround, and is that turnaround sustainable?

**Scope.** Taiwan only. January 2025 to December 2026. The unit of analysis is ETtoday's website and app, compared with the news brands TVBS新聞網, 東森新聞網, 三立新聞網, 聯合新聞網 (UDN), 自由時報電子報 (LTN) and NOWnews, and with the distribution platforms LINE TODAY, Google, Meta and YouTube. Regulators (立法院, 數位發展部, 公平交易委員會) enter as rule-setters. We do not cover TV ratings, print circulation or editorial quality.

**Objectives.**

1. Map ETtoday's Value Net and the five PARTS levers (Players, Added value, Rules, Tactics, Scope).
2. Build a dated action–response timeline for ETtoday, its rivals and the platforms, and read it through AMC (Awareness, Motivation, Capability).
3. Track platform KPIs over time: weekly use, trust, traffic mix, ad market, parent-company financials.
4. Answer the six Analysis Questions below and give ETtoday concrete advice.
5. Publish the results as a bilingual interactive web page with a two-minute video.

**Analysis Questions.**

- **AQ1 (Players, Added value).** Who are the players in ETtoday's Value Net, and where does its added value come from now that direct traffic (47.5%) and app traffic (36.3%) outweigh platform referrals?
- **AQ2 (Scope, co-opetition).** Are LINE TODAY, Google, Meta and YouTube complementors or competitors to ETtoday? Do they bring it readers or take its advertisers, and how has that balance moved since 2025?
- **AQ3 (Tactics, AMC).** When a rival or a platform makes a move, whether a new format, a promotion, a partnership or an AI product, how fast does ETtoday answer, and who usually moves first? Does the pairing of market commonality and resource similarity (Chen 1996) predict which rivals respond?
- **AQ4 (Monetisation).** How much of the 2025 turnaround came from cost (AI restructuring) and how much from revenue (digital ads, transit out-of-home media, commerce tie-ins), and what did each side of the platform give up or gain?
- **AQ5 (Rules).** How would the 媒體議價法 drafts, the 數發部 shared revenue platform and Google's NT$300 million news fund change the rules of the game for ETtoday?
- **AQ6 (Network effects).** Do cross-side network effects push Taiwan's online news market toward a single winner, or do falling trust and social-first habits fragment it, and where should ETtoday place its next bets?

**Lenses from the course.** We reuse what the class has already worked through this term rather than inventing new theory:

- *Co-opetition* (Brandenburger & Nalebuff): the Value Net with customers, suppliers, competitors and complementors around the firm, the observation that one player can be friend and foe at the same time, and the five PARTS levers. The book's closing checklist of Players, Added-value, Rules, Tactics and Scope questions is the structure of our recommendations.
- *Competitive dynamics* (Chen 1996): market commonality and resource similarity as the two dimensions that predict whether a rival notices a move and answers it, and AMC as the reading of each action–response pair.
- *Platform Revolution* (Parker, Van Alstyne & Choudary, Ch. 1–6, 9–10): cross-side network effects, multi-homing, monetisation choices, the four forces that decide whether a market tips to one winner (supply-side scale, network effects, multi-homing and switching costs, niche specialisation), and Chapter 9's warning against being "over-measured and under-prioritized", which is why the dashboard is capped at eight charts.

## 2. Chosen Firm / Platform & Strategic Rationale

**Focal platform.** ETtoday 東森新聞雲, at ettoday.net and in the ETtoday app. The operating company is 東森新媒體控股股份有限公司 (Eastern New Media Holdings), registered in 2009 as 東森新聞雲股份有限公司 and renamed in 2019, with paid-in capital of NT$600 million ([MOEA company registry](https://findbiz.nat.gov.tw/fts/company/04964250)). The news site launched in November 2011. It belongs to the 東森 group, whose listed parent is 東森國際 (TWSE 2614). Scale in 2025: 38% weekly use in DNR 2026, 28.88 million Facebook followers, direct traffic at 47.5% of visits and the company's own app at 36.3% ([東森國際 114年度年報](https://mops.twse.com.tw/mops/#/web/t146sb05?companyId=2614)). The group also reports a peak of 19 million daily visits and 6.73 million app downloads; those are self-reported and marked 待驗證 (TBD).

**The sides of the platform.**

- Readers get free, fast news on web and mobile and give attention and browsing data in return.
- Content producers: an in-house newsroom plus syndicated partners and columnists supplying text, video and vertical reports.
- Advertisers buy display, native, video and sponsored social placements, and, through the group's transit media business, out-of-home space on the Taipei Metro.
- Group tie-ins: 東森購物 and 東森寵物雲 turn attention into commerce, which matters because it reduces dependence on ad revenue that platforms skim.

**Competitors in Taiwan.** Two groups, because they play different roles. Same-side news brands: TVBS新聞網 (31% weekly use), 東森新聞網 (23%, same group but a separate brand), 三立新聞網 (20%), UDN, LTN, NOWnews and Yahoo奇摩新聞. Distribution platforms that are complementors and competitors at once: LINE TODAY (LINE is the top news gateway at 49%), Google, Meta (Facebook 47%, Threads) and YouTube (48%). All figures from [DNR 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/taiwan).

**Why ETtoday.**

- *Market position.* Nine consecutive years as Taiwan's most-used online news brand in the Reuters Institute survey, with trust well above the national average, so whatever is happening to it is happening to the market leader.
- *A visible business-model shift.* Four years of losses ended in July 2025. The group attributes this to AI-driven restructuring (561 to 385 staff) together with a move beyond programmatic ads into transit media and content commerce ([ETtoday 財經雲](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3063136)). Parent-level financials are on MOPS; ETtoday-level figures are only partly disclosed, which we flag in Section 3.
- *Network effects and multi-homing.* Readers multi-home across news apps and LINE TODAY; advertisers multi-home across news brands and the platforms. The shift to 47.5% direct traffic is a live test of whether a news platform can pull its sides back onto its own property.
- *A rule change in progress.* The 媒體議價法 drafts were sent to party negotiation on 6 August 2025 ([Taiwan News](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/zh/news/6172747)), the 數發部 is designing a shared subscription and revenue-sharing platform, Google has pledged a NT$300 million news fund, and the 公平會 has set out its position in the 數位經濟競爭政策白皮書.

**Focal event (2025–2026).** ETtoday's turnaround from four years of losses to AI-driven profit, set against a stalled bargaining law and widening losses at digital rivals such as NOWnews, which lost NT$140 million in 2025 ([LTN](https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/breakingnews/5476116)).

### 4.2 AI Agent Purpose & Scope (Intention)

The agent's job is to turn public text and documents into checkable tables. It:

1. **Collects** articles from January 2025 onward about ETtoday, the named rivals and LINE TODAY, Google, Meta and YouTube, from the outlets' public pages and RSS feeds, and official documents from the sources in Section 3.
2. **Tags** each article as one row: date, actor, action type, target, source URL, Chinese and English summary. Action types follow a fixed codebook in `KB.md`: pricing, promotion, new product or format, partnership or licensing, acquisition or investment, regulatory or legal, staffing or AI restructuring.
3. **Extracts** KPIs from the DNR, TWNIC, DMA and MOPS documents into a tidy table: brand, year, metric, value, source; and pulls the series in Section 3.3 that have an API or a sitemap (YouTube channel activity, articles per month, app reviews tagged by theme).
4. **Computes** action counts by actor and quarter, response lag in days between a move and the first reply by another actor, KPI trends across years, and a first-pass score of each rival on market commonality and resource similarity for the Strategy pair to adjust.
5. **Drafts** the PARTS table, the AMC reading of each action–response pair, and bilingual text for the site, all for members to edit.

Two rules bound it: nothing reaches the site without a member's name in the `checked_by` column, and no figure appears without a source URL. Every change to the prompts or `KB.md` gets a dated version line.

### 4.3 Periodic Data Update Plan

Target: a weekly run of the collection step that appends candidate rows to the staging tab for review, and a site rebuild on every push once rows are verified. Whether the weekly run is a scheduled task or started by hand is 待驗證 (TBD) until the first full pipeline test in W11.

### 4.4 GitHub Pages Deployment

One public repository (name 待驗證 (TBD)). Layout: `data/` for the exported JSON, `src/` for the Vue app with `zh-TW` and `en` content files, `KB.md` for prompts, codebook and version log, and a GitHub Actions workflow that runs `vite build` and publishes `dist/` to the `gh-pages` branch. ### 5.1 The interactive single-page application

A Vue single-page application, bilingual (zh-TW / en toggle in the header, every string in both languages), responsive for phone and desktop, reading all data from JSON files in the repository. Each section of the page is built around one framework from the course, so a visitor who knows the frameworks can read the page as an argument and a visitor who does not can read it as a story. Top to bottom:

1. **Opening.** The research question in one sentence and a strip of five headline numbers: 38% weekly use, 46% trust, 47.5% direct traffic, 36.3% app traffic, July 2025 return to profit. Each number links to its source.
2. **The game board: Value Net (AQ1, AQ2).** ETtoday in the centre; readers, advertisers, content partners, rival brands, distribution platforms and regulators around it, in the four Value Net roles from *Co-opetition*. Clicking a player opens a panel with its role, what flows to and from ETtoday, and our friend-or-foe verdict (complementor, competitor or both), with the evidence rows behind it.
3. **Competitor map: market commonality × resource similarity (AQ3).** A 2×2 scatter in the style of Chen (1996): each rival and platform placed by how much audience and platform space it shares with ETtoday and how similar its resources are. Hovering a rival shows the AMC prediction (how likely it is to notice and answer ETtoday's moves) next to what the timeline actually recorded. This is the bridge between the theory sessions and the data.
4. **Action–response timeline, 2025–2026 (AQ3, AMC).** One lane per actor, dots coloured by action type, and connectors from a move to the responses it drew, with the lag in days on the connector. Filters by actor, action type and quarter; clicking a dot shows the bilingual summary and the source link. A small panel reports who moved first most often and the median response lag by action type. Dragging across the timeline sets the date window for every chart below it.
5. **Platform dashboard (AQ1, AQ4, AQ6).** Eight charts and no more, following Chapter 9 of *Platform Revolution*: (a) ETtoday's traffic mix 2017–2025 as a stacked area; (b) ETtoday revenue, profit and headcount by year; (c) weekly use and trust by brand 2017–2026; (d) Google Trends attention by brand with timeline events marked; (e) articles published per month per outlet against headcount; (f) YouTube uploads and Shorts share per channel; (g) digital ad spend by format and by general vs social media; (h) app rating and review themes by month. Hover for values, toggle series, brush a window, and every chart carries its source and licence.
6. **Rules panel (AQ5).** A dated sequence of the 媒體議價法 reviews, the 數發部 shared-subscription plan and the Google fund, one line each on what it changes for ETtoday, and a scenario slider: if a fund of NT$X were shared by usage, what would ETtoday's share be using DNR weekly-use shares as the proxy? Labelled as a scenario, not a forecast.
7. **Insights.** Six cards, one per AQ, each with a two-paragraph answer, the chart or timeline view it rests on, and a link to the verified rows. The AQ6 card runs the market through the four winner-take-all forces from *Platform Revolution* Chapter 10 and says whether Taiwan's online news tips or fragments. The section closes with recommendations to ETtoday arranged by the PARTS checklist from *Co-opetition* Chapter 9: one recommendation each on Players, Added value, Rules, Tactics and Scope.
8. **Video.** The two-minute bilingual video embedded from YouTube.
9. **Footer.** AI-use disclosure (which tools, for which steps, reviewed by whom), source list with licences and access dates, team, course materials, and a link to the repository.

What visitors can do: switch language, filter, brush and click the timeline, move the scenario slider, inspect any value, open any source. What they cannot do: log in, submit data, or trigger a live scrape. The page is deliberately static so that it stays up with no server and no keys.
