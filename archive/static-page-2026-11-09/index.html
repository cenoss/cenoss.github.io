The Two Layers of Taiwan's News Business

Newsrooms such as ETtoday, TVBS and UDN produce the news; LINE, YouTube and Facebook decide who sees it. ETtoday runs a two-sided platform of its own while also supplying the larger platforms.

  • LINE 49%, YouTube 48% and Facebook 47% are used for news
  • NT$66.2 billion digital ad market in 2025
  • Its growth flows to international platforms

What Does the Project Leave Out?

TV ratings, print circulation and editorial quality. The project covers ETtoday's website and app in Taiwan from January 2025 to December 2026.

Is Every ETtoday Figure Verified?

Not yet. ETtoday's 19 M peak daily visits and 6.73 M app downloads are self-reported, and the site labels both accordingly.

How Is the Work Divided?

Among three pairs: Data (sources and verification), Strategy Analysis (financials, PARTS and the Value Net), and Web and Video (the site, the video and GitHub Pages).

How Does Group 4 Use AI?

Claude helped draft and edit parts of the proposal and this page. Group members checked every fact against its source, and the final text is the group's own.
